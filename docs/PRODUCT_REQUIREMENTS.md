# Product requirements

Status: Proposed baseline. Owner approval is required for scope-defining changes.

## Product

Acadify is an India-focused hyperlocal learning marketplace for online and offline learning. Learners find and book verified tutors, college mentors, and professionals. Educators offer sessions and publish learning resources. Brand promise: **Learn Better. Teach Smarter.**

## Audiences and roles

- **Learner/student:** needs trusted, relevant help with a subject or skill, quickly and at a clear price.
- **Educator/tutor:** needs a professional profile, manageable availability, a clear way to handle enquiries and bookings, and ways to earn from teaching and content.
- **Marketplace admin:** reviews verification claims and moderates unsafe or unsuitable content.

The demo shall support role switching without real identity, authorization, or verification claims.

## Core journeys

1. Discover educators by subject, mode (online/offline), location, price, availability, language, and rating; inspect a profile and request a session.
2. Use a short preference flow to receive tutor matches, with visible reasons and clear separation between AI suggestions and verified facts.
3. View a session schedule, exchange demo messages, and see booking states.
4. Browse educator resources and complete a simulated checkout that is clearly labeled as a demo.
5. Ask a study assistant for an explanation or practice questions, with an isolated mock response/provider boundary.
6. Switch to educator/admin demo views to manage availability, content, bookings, verification queue, and moderation.
7. Educator reviews new requests, accepts or declines locally, publishes a resource to the learner library, and checks an illustrative earnings breakdown.

## Initial category focus

Class 11–12 PCM, coding mentorship, and spoken English. The longer-term concept includes home/online tuition, assignment support, interview preparation, music, dance, yoga, drawing, AI tools, resume review, and college counselling. Wider categories remain seeded as future-ready taxonomy, not a promise of launch coverage.

## Product principles

- Trust indicators must state what is actually verified; demo badges must say “Demo”.
- AI output is advisory, explainable, and never presented as a credential, rating, or guarantee.
- Show price, session mode, location context, and booking status before commitment.
- Give educators a useful workspace for the day’s requests, schedule, profile performance, availability, and creator work.
- Mobile-first, accessible, responsive UI; plain language and respectful learner safety.
- Use synthetic sample data only until a separately approved privacy and operations plan exists.

## Success criteria for portfolio demo

- A reviewer can complete the learner discovery-to-booking journey without external accounts.
- Tutor profiles, filters, profile detail, scheduling, messages, creator content, and simulated checkout are coherent and usable.
- Tutor and admin demo surfaces demonstrate the marketplace supply and trust loops.
- AI matching and study-assistant behavior work as labeled mock flows, with transparent reasons and a replaceable integration seam.
- Product limits, implementation status, and non-integrations are visible in project documentation.
